 📊 Executive Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyse retail sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour and regional performance.

---
📌 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate thousands of sales transactions every year. Decision-makers need a single dashboard to monitor business performance, identify profitable products, understand customer segments and make informed strategic decisions.

This dashboard provides executives with key performance indicators and interactive visualisations for analysing sales performance across multiple business dimensions.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profitability
- Identify top-performing products
- Discover high-value customers
- Evaluate regional performance
- Analyse shipping methods
- Understand customer segmentation
- Measure the impact of discounts on profit

---

🛠 Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modelling

 💡 Business Insights

- Technology category generates the highest sales.
- Consumer segment contributes the largest share of revenue.
- Higher discounts often reduce profitability.
- Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping mode.
- A small group of customers contributes a significant portion of total sales.
